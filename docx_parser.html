DOCX Parser

Standalone, offline .docx text extractor. Everything runs in your browser with plain JavaScript — no uploads, no network, no server, no Python.

Drop a .docx file here, or click to browse
Output:
Parses the WordprocessingML inside the .docx (a ZIP archive): body paragraphs, tables, list markers, headers, footers, footnotes, endnotes, and core document properties. Tracked-change deletions and field codes are skipped. Images, embedded objects, and formatting are not extracted.
